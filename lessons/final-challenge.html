📋 Scenario

You are the lead ML engineer at a startup. Your task: train, align, and serve a 70B-parameter LLM for a customer-support chatbot grounded in the company's internal documentation. Your hardware: 1024 H100 GPUs (128 nodes × 8 GPUs each, NVLink intra-node, InfiniBand inter-node). Budget: a few weeks.

The questions below walk you through the whole pipeline. Attempt every blank before opening the solution.
